## Project Overview
This project analyzes electricity consumption and generation across U.S. states to understand the main factors that influence electricity demand and the differences in electricity production between states. Using publicly available datasets, the project will combine electricity, population, economic, climate, generation, and energy-source data to identify patterns and relationships.

---

## Project Objectives
- Analyze and compare electricity consumption across U.S. states and identify states with the highest total and per-capita consumption.
- Investigate the main factors associated with electricity demand, including population, GDP, industrial activity, electricity prices, and climate conditions.
- Analyze electricity generation by energy source and compare generation capacity with actual electricity production.

- Compare electricity generation with consumption to identify states that generate more electricity than they consume and states that depend more heavily on external electricity supply.
- Analyze changes in electricity consumption, generation, and energy mix over time.
- Evaluate renewable energy adoption and the environmental impact of electricity generation across states.
- Identify meaningful patterns, relationships, and outliers and translate them into key insights.

---

## Project Scope
- Electricity consumption by state, sector, and year.
- Electricity generation by state and energy source.
- Population and population growth.
- Industrial, residential, and commercial electricity consumption.
- Electricity prices.
- Renewable energy share and changes in the energy mix.
- Comparison between electricity generation and consumption.
